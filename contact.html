Lily Chan is a Malaysian-Chinese multidisciplinary creative. She has a strong interest in exploring themes of cultural & queer identities, music, fashion, mental health, and the human affliction. Lover of all things maximalist, colourful, and bizarre.

She has gained professional graphic design & illustration experience working at Mossery, Nuttshell, and freelancing . She is currently employed at Smash Products as a Junior Graphic Designer & Illustrator.

She graduated from the University of Melbourne with a Bachelor's degree in Design, major in Graphic Design, and specialisation in Typography.
